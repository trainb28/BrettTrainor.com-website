About Brett

Six years ago I was pushed out of a corporate career I'd spent two decades building. Instead of finding my next job, I figured out how to build income without a boss — consulting, coaching, community, content — and then started helping others do the same.

I'm a GenX guy from the Chicago suburbs. Married, dad of three daughters, new grandfather. I believe leaving corporate is tactical but breaking the psychological dependency is the harder work — and the more important one.

The Corporate Escapee ecosystem — podcast, community, training, advisory — exists because I wish it had existed when I needed it.
