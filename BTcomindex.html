GenX guy from Naperville, Illinois. Husband, dad of three daughters, proud grandpa. Over three decades I have led GTM roles at big corporate, started and run small companies — from Bagel Cafes to Mobile Technology, Digital Agencies, Consulting, and a service-centric business.

Good people who were really good at their jobs deserve to win on their own terms.

I have coached and mentored hundreds of business owners and corporate escapees, led sales, marketing, customer success, demand generation and operations for mid-market and global companies, and helped grow a startup from $100K to $14M+ in under two years. Six years ago I left corporate and never looked back.

Today The Corporate Escapee is a media brand and community ecosystem helping mid-career professionals (40-55) build solopreneur businesses around the 6Fs: Financial Stability, Friends and Family, Freedom, Fitness, Fulfillment, and Fun — with 110K+ followers, 10K+ subscribers, and a top 2.5% podcast.
